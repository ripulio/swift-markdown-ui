# Fork Provenance & Maintenance Notes

`ripulio/swift-markdown-ui` is an actively maintained, source-compatible continuation of [`gonzalezreal/swift-markdown-ui`](https://github.com/gonzalezreal/swift-markdown-ui), maintained by [Ripul](https://ripul.io).

- **Upstream base:** MarkdownUI **`2.4.1`** (`5f613358148239d0292c0cef674a3c2314737f9e`)
- **License & attribution:** Upstream MIT license and copyright notice remain intact in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE).
- **Default branch:** `ripul` (consumers should pin a semantic version tag such as `from: "2.5.1"`).
